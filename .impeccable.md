## Design Context

### Users

The primary audience is AI engineers, technical educators, and engineering leaders who want a rigorous map of prompt, context, and harness engineering. They use the page as an authoritative public syllabus and durable reference: to understand the field's conceptual structure, find canonical readings, design a course or study plan, and return to specific modules over time. The page is not primarily a lead-generation funnel or a conventional course-sales landing page.

### Brand Personality

Rigorous, editorial, quietly distinctive. The voice should feel intellectually confident, evidence-driven, and written by a practitioner with a point of view. It should avoid sales language, trend-chasing, breathless futurism, and generic AI-product aesthetics.

### Aesthetic Direction

A warm, bookish, light-mode editorial experience: closer to a carefully designed long-form syllabus, technical monograph, or independent magazine than a SaaS marketing site. Serif-led reading typography, restrained sans-serif interface text, subtle rules, and meaningful color-coding should support long sessions and repeated reference use. Avoid neon-on-dark palettes, gradients, glassmorphism, generic card grids, oversized marketing metrics, and decorative technical imagery.

### Design Principles

1. Make intellectual structure immediately legible: the prompt -> context -> harness -> agent progression is the page's memorable organizing idea.
2. Optimize for deep reading and repeated lookup, not conversion theater; navigation and hierarchy should help readers re-enter a 12,000-plus-word document efficiently.
3. Preserve editorial calm while creating enough rhythm and compression to prevent the curriculum from becoming a wall of equally weighted modules.
4. Earn authority through primary sources, clear distinctions, critical counterpoints, and concrete labs rather than promotional claims.
5. Keep the page self-contained, fast, accessible, printable, and visually distinctive without adding a framework or ornamental complexity.
